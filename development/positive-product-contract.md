# Positive Product Contract

Status: Provisional product truth under Sam Walker's end-to-end build mandate.

## Product

OmniView: The Looking Glass Companion is a respectful enhancement of Pliny the Liberator's GL4SS. It preserves GL4SS as Pliny's authored web instrument while giving its strongest idea - a wormhole camera aimed through place and time - a more capable semantic engine and an agent-native companion.

## Desired world-change

A GL4SS maintainer can replace two bounded prompt surfaces and obtain more grounded, more evocative, more model-portable scene planning and render language without replacing the application's interaction model or provider pipeline.

An agent user can name or explore a place, time, hour, and visual intention and receive:

1. a grounded scene interpretation that distinguishes observation, historical or palaeoecological inference, and speculation;
2. a high-density render prompt whose diction, composition, materials, light, atmosphere, and temporal character provoke the intended image;
3. an image, comparison, or temporal traversal when the host exposes suitable image tools;
4. a complete prompt-only handoff when image tools are unavailable.

## Deliberate handoff objects

This is one source-custody project with two deliberately separate peer handoffs:

1. **GL4SS drop-in upgrade pack**
   - a scene-planner prompt replacement preserving the current SceneDirection contract where evidence permits;
   - a render-language module or template replacement preserving the existing image/video call boundary where evidence permits;
   - integration notes, contract tests, representative examples, and an evidence-bounded comparison.

2. **OmniView companion Augment**
   - one coherent customer kit;
   - self-contained Codex and Claude distributions;
   - host-image operation when available and prompt-only degradation when absent;
   - attribution and provenance that make Pliny's GL4SS relationship explicit without presenting the companion as an official replacement.

## Requested terminal state

A private, build-complete, documented, and verification-reviewed peer handoff. Publication, a remote repository, upstream submission, installation, public claims, and messages to Pliny remain separately authorized.

## Acceptance evidence

- Every drop-in names its exact GL4SS seam and contract impact.
- Representative cases cover dense, sparse, traces-only, uninhabited, deep-time, exact-event, and speculative-future scenes.
- Baseline and candidate prompts can be compared without model spend.
- Paid image or video A/Bs occur only with separate authority and record the exact model, prompt, settings, outputs, cost, and human judgment.
- Improvements are tested for grounding, temporal differentiation, habitation, compositional specificity, material/aesthetic specificity, attention density, portability, and degraded behavior.
- Codex and Claude distributions validate structurally and remain self-contained.
- Live installation, activation, image-tool execution, and cross-host behavior are claimed only where separately observed.
- TestForge and Hesperos remain independent completion gates before a done-done handoff.

## Boundaries

- Preserve GL4SS rather than silently redesigning it.
- Produce alternatives and patches; do not mutate Pliny's checkout.
- Use no stored browser key, credential, paid provider call, publication channel, or upstream repository without separate authority.
- Treat exact historical and palaeoecological claims as evidence-sensitive, with uncertainty visible where the available corpus cannot settle them.
- Keep v1 portable: host image capability plus prompt-only fallback. A direct OpenRouter adapter is a later option unless evidence shows it is necessary to prove the product.
