# Decision 0001 - Witness to Shot Relay

Status: Accepted for the first implementation.

## Decision

Preserve GL4SS's single SceneDirection planning call and deterministic prompt compiler. Replace the planner's accumulated prohibition stack with a positive, enacted observation sequence, then improve the deterministic language that turns its existing fields into a shot.

The relay is:

1. locate the coordinate and temporal resolution;
2. read the ground and the life or work upon it as evidence;
3. choose the habitation state;
4. find the visible change that distinguishes this year from its neighbours;
5. place a physically compatible optic;
6. compose distinct focal events across depth;
7. express exact materials, actions, surfaces, atmosphere, and light in image-native language;
8. return the existing JSON shape.

The prompt will not merely specify accuracy, atmosphere, or specificity as desired outcomes. It will place the model behind the wormhole aperture and give it a sequence of perceptual acts that naturally produces them.

## Why this architecture

GL4SS already owns the correct application boundaries and most of the right evidence fields. Its main semantic failure is that the planner is taught through a growing catalogue of wrong answers, while the renderer receives correct facts as adjacent paragraphs rather than one image-bearing scene.

The Witness to Shot Relay:

- keeps Pliny's instrument recognizably his;
- changes no provider, engine, storage, UI, or public TypeScript contract;
- gives every retained instruction a positive job inside the model's performance;
- increases image-language density without adding another model call;
- remains falsifiable at the planner, parser, compiler, and rendered-image layers.

## Promptcraft commitments

- Identity is functional: historian, field ecologist, material-culture reader, and still photographer operate as one eye.
- The coordinate and date are a scene to inhabit, not parameters to restate.
- Exact nouns earn their place by producing visible difference.
- Habitation is expressed as a positive spatial condition.
- The current ground, water, ice, growth, structure lifecycle, and camera support date the frame together.
- The output schema is a compositional vessel, not the prompt's governing metaphor.
- Negative facts remain in source-card oracles and deterministic gates whenever naming them could recruit the wrong image prior.
- Output restrictions are stated as the form the response takes.

## Alternatives deferred

### New renderDirection field

An integrated render sentence could increase ceiling, but it enlarges a token-sensitive schema and may duplicate or contradict separately structured facts. Reconsider only if deterministic weaving leaves a measured integration gap.

### Second art-director call

A second generative pass adds latency, spend, provider coupling, failure states, and another reconstruction of the world. It is not justified before the existing information is made to perform.

### Replacement application

A new frontend or rival visualization product violates the relationship boundary. The companion Augment is agent-native, but it does not present itself as a replacement GL4SS.

## Falsifiers

Reject or revise this decision if the candidate:

- increases JSON parse or fallback failures;
- loses any of the four parser-required strings;
- weakens habitation behavior in dense, traces-only, or uninhabited sentinels;
- makes neighbouring time stations less differentiable;
- places the viewpoint on a surface absent in that year;
- drops non-empty planner facts during default compilation;
- weakens custom-template preservation;
- needs a public contract change to produce its claimed benefit.

## Evidence boundary

The current GL4SS checkout is the control. Repository comments describe earlier live experiments, but the underlying prompts, outputs, and receipts are not preserved as a complete release baseline. This project will make bounded current-control-versus-candidate claims only.
