**View**

From a sun-struck quay at Misenum, eye level 1.7 metres above worn volcanic paving, the Bay of Naples opens eastward in a broad blue arc. A naval galley’s oar blades rise unevenly beside the harbor wall while sailors work the lines; terraced villas and pale settlements gather through heat haze along the far shore, with Vesuvius holding the horizon. White limestone, red tile, bronze fittings, linen tunics, and wet hull planks throw sharp noon reflections as cross-bay wind wrinkles the water.
