**Render prompt**

Wide documentary photograph from physical footing on a worn stone quay at Misenum, AD 79, 12:03 local time. An oared Roman naval craft turns off-centre through the bright Bay of Naples, wet dark hull flashing as sailors pull in staggered rhythm; coiled hemp rope, salt-whitened quay stones, amphorae, and a mooring post occupy the foreground. Maintained tiled roofs, harbor workshops, merchant hulls, mastheads, and working people overlap through the middle distance of the Roman naval base. Across the bay, Vesuvius rises above cultivated lower slopes and pale coastal settlements. High southern midday sun rakes the oar-blades, scatters glare across the water, and leaves compact blue shadows beneath awnings; sea haze veils the far shore, with an off-centre horizon and natural depth from quay to harbor to mountain.

**Grounding**

AD 79 plus a local hour supports an ordinary Roman naval-base scene, rather than a specific eruption phase. The exact day and season remain unresolved; the local noon geometry and ordinary maintained harbor activity are bounded visual inference. Render state: prompt-only.
