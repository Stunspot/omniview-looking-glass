**View**

At first light, Uruk is already awake: porters thread between reed baskets and newly plastered mudbrick walls, while a worker presses damp clay against a cone-mosaic façade. The low sun catches the lime surface and wet canal margins, leaving the packed-earth lane in cool shadow beneath the rising terraces.
