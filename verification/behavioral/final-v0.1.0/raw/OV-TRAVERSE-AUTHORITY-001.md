### Invariant optic

The default is a **world-fixed aperture**: one latitude/longitude, absolute aperture elevation, bearing, angle of view, wide documentary frame, and broad midday illumination held across all five stops. This preserves geometry if the shoreline advances, retreats, submerges the former footing, or exposes new ground. If the named coast has a bedrock surface demonstrably present at every stop, the traversal can instead use a **physical-footing invariant**.

The coast answer unlocks the coordinate meaning, aperture elevation, bearing, persistent anchor, ground reconstruction, habitation state, and the complete five prompts.
