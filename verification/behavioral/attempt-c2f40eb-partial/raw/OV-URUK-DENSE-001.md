Wide documentary frame, Uruk around 3200 BC at first light, from human eye level on a packed-clay lane beside a reed-lined canal. A water carrier braces a shoulder pole as damp jars swing above the rutted ground; a porter pivots a reed basket around a freshly repaired mudbrick corner, while seal cutters open a workshop threshold and workers guide donkeys through the widening street. Dense mudbrick walls, bitumen-darkened drainage edges, reed mat awnings, clay tablets and coils of fiber occupy the near ground; smoke lifts from roofline hearths through the middle distance toward temple platforms rising beyond the clustered city. Dawn light enters low along the canal, rakes damp plaster and jar shoulders, turns suspended dust pale gold between the buildings, and pools cool blue shadow beneath awnings; wide 16:9 composition, off-centre motion, layered foreground-to-horizon depth, fine documentary grain.
